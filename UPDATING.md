# Keeping the site fresh

Everything below happens in the editing screen at **`/studio`** on the live
site (bookmark it). Changes go live a minute or two after you hit **Publish**.
Nothing here requires touching code.

## When the new monthly flyer drops (~15 minutes)

1. Open **`/studio` → Events**.
2. For each thing on the flyer, click **＋** and fill in: title, date & start
   time, end time if it has one, and a category (music / art / games / party /
   community — it controls the little colored tag).
3. Skip anything that happens every week — that lives under **Weekly events**
   instead, and only needs editing when the regular lineup changes.
4. Publish each one. Done — past events disappear from the site automatically,
   so there's nothing to clean up from last month.

## The announcement banner (check weekly)

**Studio → Site Settings → Name & Contact → Announcement banner.**
This is the colored strip across the very top of the site. Great for "closed
today", "anniversary party Friday", "new flyer is up". **Clear it when the
news is over** — a stale banner is worse than no banner.

## Hours, phone, links

**Studio → Site Settings.** Hours live under the Hours tab (type times like
`12:00 PM` — the "open now" pill on the site reads them, so keep that format).
Tap count, the on-tap intro, about text, and the offer cards each have their
own tab.

## Photos

**Studio → Gallery.** Upload a photo, write one line describing it (this is
read aloud to blind visitors, so describe what's in the picture), publish.
The gallery section only appears on the site once at least one photo exists.

## If something looks wrong

The site keeps an emergency copy of all content baked in — if Sanity is ever
unreachable it quietly serves that instead, so the page never goes blank.
If an edit doesn't show up after a few minutes, make sure you hit **Publish**
(not just save) and give the page a hard refresh.
